About

I graduated from the University of Toronto Mississauga in October 2026 with an Honours BSc in Psychology and minors in Computer Science and Mathematics. Over two years at Environment and Climate Change Canada, I worked as a business analyst on an information-sharing initiative, tested Weather Event Layer features, and documented systems used for nationwide weather forecasting.

I like the space between people and systems: finding out what is actually getting in someone's way, then designing or specifying something that fixes it.
