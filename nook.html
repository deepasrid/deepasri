Nook: finding third spaces near campus

A mobile app concept that helps university students discover and access local places to study, relax and meet people outside home and class.

The problem

Students often struggle to find good places off campus to study, work or spend time, and to know whether those places are open, welcoming and accessible to them. We set out to understand why discovering and accessing local third spaces is harder than it should be.

Research

  • Interviews and surveys with 30+ university students.
  • Conversations with 15 local third spaces to understand their side of the experience.
  • Focus: the core pain points in discovering and accessing community spaces.

Design process

I started with low-fidelity paper prototypes and ran an initial round of usability testing to check navigation flows and information architecture before investing in visual design. I then built an interactive high-fidelity prototype in Figma using what the paper tests showed, and tested it again.
